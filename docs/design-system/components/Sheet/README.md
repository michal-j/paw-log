A bottom sheet for jump-to-date, filters and add flows.

White, `radius-sheet` on the top corners, `shadow-sheet`, 16px side padding, a 36 by 4px grabber in `line-strong`. A scrim of `ink` at about 30% covers the whole screen behind it (so the rounded corners show the dimmed page, never white) and fades in 180ms; the sheet rises in 220ms. Inputs are 50px high, 16px text (stops iOS zoom), `radius-md`, `surface-tint` fill, `line-strong` border, a 3px `mint` focus glow. Max height about 640px; the body scrolls, the heading and action row stay. The sheet closes on Close, Escape, a tap on the scrim, or a drag of the grabber or heading downwards (past about 90px, or a quick flick; a shorter drag springs back; the body is not draggable, so it scrolls as usual). The consumer provides the heading, body and actions.

A filter sheet (Search): the heading can carry "Clear" in place of "Close" (grey and inactive while nothing is chosen), the body is captioned groups of toggle Chips, and the one action is a primary button whose label carries a live count ("Show results (12)") so the effect of a toggle shows before closing. The scrim, Escape and that button close it.
